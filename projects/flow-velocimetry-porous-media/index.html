Particle tracking velocimetry

Measuring flow fields in both porous media and turbulent flows.

Deformation statistics of porous media

We used particle tracking velocimetry to measure the deformation of solute elements in porous media with varying heterogeneity—fabricated by 3D printing. A random-walk model including deformation from the velocity-gradient tensor only in near-wall regions successfully describes the elongation of material lines. The elongation we describe accelerates fluid mixing and sets the rates of chemical reactions between substances in the flow.

  • Pierce, J.K., Le Borgne, T., Renard, F., & Linga, G., How pore-scale disorder controls fluid stretching in porous media, arXiv:2604.02981, 2026. In revision for Journal of Fluid Mechanics.

Resolving Haines jumps in multiphase flow

Fluid interfaces between air and water phases move in an intermittent fashion in porous media due to the competition between viscous and capillary forces during flow. At high capillary numbers (to the left), the flow is continuous, while at lower capillary numbers (moving right) it becomes increasingly bursty and intermittent.
