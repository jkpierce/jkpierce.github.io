3D-printed millifluidic flow cells

Transparent flow cells with adaptable geometries, made fast and in bulk.

  • Dumay, Y., Pierce, J.K., Delhaie, J., & Eiser, E., Transparent 3D-printed flow cells for porous media studies with proof-of-concept on two-phase flow-reversal asymmetry, arXiv:2610.04105, 2026.

Three-dimensional granular porous media

Some early trials of 3D printing realistic granular samples. Angular polyhedra are packed using Yade, and we manage to 3D print these after some trial and error. Refractive index matching remains challenging due to refractive index heterogeneities.

CNC + assembly

Some experiment components with milled PVC and PMMA components, bonded with UV-curable glue and sealed with water-jetted gaskets.

Optics and computer automation

Custom optical system + instruments fully coordinated with scripts and triggers.
