To date my teaching work has mainly involved teaching assistantships and course content development.

Course Development

During my PhD I gained a lot of experience incorporating emerging virtual technologies into university courses:

  • Developed five laboratory assignments with solutions and introductory materials for a fourth year undergraduate Fluvial Geomorphology course.

  • Produced a 45min geomorphology ``Virtual Field Trip" video documentary involving a tour of the UBC Research Forest and pedagogical explanations of various features.

  • Contributed to a virtual field trip phone application for a Climate and Ecosystems course. Provided several short video explanations of ecology concepts.

  • Developed five laboratory assignments for an introductory Geomorphology course. Assignments are oriented around a photo tour of the Tahoma watershed on Mount Rainier which was implemented in Tapestry. Questions are hosted in Canvas.

  • Developed five laboratory assignments for a Statistics in Geography course. Assignments are hosted in Github and require the application of R to solve statistical analysis problems using jupyter. Assignments are auto-graded using nb-grader. Topics include data management, regression analysis, t-testing, analysis of variance, and chi-squared analysis.
