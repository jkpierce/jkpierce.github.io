Personal Information

I am a PhD student in Geography at the University of British Columbia, in Vancouver, Canada. Previously I earned an MSc in physics at University of British Columbia and a BS in physics at West Virginia University. My hobbies include backpacking, mountain biking, and whitewater kayaking. I welcome any emails to discuss science or invitations to give talks.

Research Interests

In my research I study sediment transport processes on Earth’s surface using mechanistic physics. My work consists of analytical modeling, laboratory experiments, and computational studies.

* Analytical modeling

I describe the movements of individual grains probabilistically using ideas from kinetic theory and the physics of active matter.

* Experimental studies

I conduct experiments on sediment transport processes in river channels with emphasis on fluid turbulence and grain-scale processes.

* Computational physics

I produce discrete element simulations of sediment transport involving non-spherical particles.
