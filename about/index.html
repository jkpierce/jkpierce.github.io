Personal Information:

I am a researcher at Porelab and the Njord Centre at the University of Oslo, in the departments of physics and geosciences.

My research aims to understand and predict how fluids, chemicals, and sediments move through the environment, from pore-scale mixing and reactions in porous media to bedload transport in rivers.

I completed an MSc in condensed matter physics and a PhD in geomorphology and hydrology at the University of British Columbia in Canada. My work spans laboratory experiments, numerical simulations, and analytical modeling.

Research Interests:

  • Fluid dynamics in porous media
  • Solute mixing and chemical reactions
  • Multi-phase flows in porous media
  • Sediment transport mechanics
  • Applied stochastic processes
